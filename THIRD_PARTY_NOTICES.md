## Bro skill

The optional native `bro` skill is adapted from [`luchasarie/bro-skill`, commit `6c08e7ee5eb8ef8b9bf0588d400170654abc256b`](https://github.com/luchasarie/bro-skill/tree/6c08e7ee5eb8ef8b9bf0588d400170654abc256b).
